This public page shows how ASAP Appliance customers provide consent to receive SMS messages for appliance repair scheduling, appointment updates, estimates, invoices, technician arrival updates, and service follow-ups.

Customers opt in by checking the SMS consent checkbox before submitting the service request form. The SMS consent checkbox is optional, unchecked by default, and consent is not required to purchase services.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

ASAP Appliance respects customer privacy. Information submitted through this form, including name, phone number, service address, and appliance/service issue, is used only to respond to customer inquiries, schedule appliance repair services, provide estimates, send invoices, coordinate technician arrival, and follow up regarding service.

ASAP Appliance does not sell, rent, or share customer mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Customer information may be shared only with service providers or technicians when necessary to perform requested appliance repair services, process communications, or complete business operations related to the customer’s service request.

Customers may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message from ASAP Appliance. Customers may reply HELP for assistance.

SMS Terms

SMS Terms

By checking the SMS consent box before submitting the service request form, the customer agrees to receive SMS text messages from ASAP Appliance at the phone number provided.

  • SMS messages may include scheduling, appointment updates, estimates, invoices, technician arrival updates, and service follow-ups.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Additional Information

Customers opt in to receive SMS messages from ASAP Appliance through the service request form at https://asapacboss.com/confirmations. The form collects the customer’s name, phone number, service address, and appliance/service issue. Before submitting the form, the customer may voluntarily check an unchecked SMS consent checkbox agreeing to receive SMS messages from ASAP Appliance about scheduling, appointment updates, estimates, invoices, technician arrival updates, and service follow-ups. The checkbox language states that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, customers can reply STOP to opt out, reply HELP for help, and that consent is not a condition of purchase. SMS consent is not preselected and is not required to submit a service request.
